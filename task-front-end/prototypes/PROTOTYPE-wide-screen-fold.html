What to look for

  • Q10 in the flesh. At 1500 the cap of 5 leaves a hole in the second row. Walk the widths with the same scenario: does the hole read as restraint or as broken layout? Try the ragged-slot toggle — outlining it is the cheapest possible answer if it reads as breakage.
  • Q14 — four collapsed bands, no recommendation. Walk them in order.
    count only — honest and silent; you must open the door to learn anything.
    count + stripes — colour tells you nothing in there is red, without words.
    count + words — "nothing urgent · soonest in 11 days". Says what the stripes only imply, and it is the only variant that can tell you when the next thing arrives.
    stripes + words — everything, and possibly too much for a band whose job is to be shut.
    The test is not which is prettiest: it is which one you could look at and then not click.
  • The cost of any of them. This is a second thing to read on a screen whose whole point is that it shows five things. If a variant makes you feel surveyed rather than reassured, count-only wins.
  • Expanding inside a grid. Click any panel at 1500. The cell grows and its row-mates stay top-aligned, so nothing below jumps — but the row does get taller. This is the one genuinely new problem the grid creates; ADR-0006 decided the panel before the grid existed.
  • Truncation. ADR-0006 rejected always-visible buttons because names truncated at three columns. Check the names still fit without those buttons: "Learn 'Wish You Were Here' intro" is the worst case in this set.
