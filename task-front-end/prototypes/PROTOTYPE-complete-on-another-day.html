What to look for

  • Does swipe stay honest? In every variant a swipe-right writes completedOn = today with nothing in your way. That is ADR-0014's line — the question is only whether the deliberate case has somewhere to go.
  • A — is the toast row enough, or does it get missed? The backdate lives inside the 8s horizon. After that the completion is fixed and undo-then-redo is the only way back (ADR-0011). Is "I did it Saturday" a thing you realise within 8 seconds of the swipe?
  • B — is a split button one affordance too many? The panel already carries Edit, Postpone ▾, Complete, Cancel. Complete ▾ makes five. Compare against how rarely you actually backdate a one-off (vs a template, where every completion is "when?").
  • C — is one modal on every deliberate complete worth the consistency? Swipe is still free; only the tapped verb asks. This is the smallest diff from today's "one confirm" design. Flip A→C a few times and feel the friction difference.
  • The template clock. Backdate a completion of Vacuum the living room and the next fire-window jumps earlier with it (ADR-0022). Is that ever a surprise, or always what you meant?
  • Only "Vacuum…" is from a template. The other four are hand-made — their completedOn is read by nothing but history. Maybe backdating only ever matters for generated tasks, and that narrows the surface.
